## Section 5 (360 benchmark networks)
- **Table 4.** Six-family ranks and wins come from `model_comparison_summary_all.csv`. Seven-model ranks, wins and RelMSE summaries come from `seven_model_summary_all.csv`. Intervals are the non-stratified network-bootstrap intervals (`*_nb`).
- **Table 5a.** EN−EBM, EN−fastai and EBM−fastai come from `pairwise_effect_sizes_all.csv`; EN−EBM is obtained by inverting the stored EBM−EN row. OLS−EN is computed directly and has no bootstrap interval. It is marked "extension".
- **Table 5b.** Median and mean RelMSE by subset for EN, EBM, fastai and OLS.
- **Figure 2.** 110 OLS values below 1e-8 are drawn as open markers at the floor.
- **Figure 3.** Times are one-core cluster wall-clock times. OLS was timed on a local laptop core (median 0.03 s), so it is shown as an open marker with a footnote and is not comparable. EBM has the lowest median RelMSE; EN has the best mean rank. The title states both.

## Section 6
- **Table 6.** H1 and H2 estimates, intervals and Holm p are read from `inference.csv`. Subset slopes for H1 are separate OLS fits per subset. The pooled H1 slope uses subset intercepts; refitting reproduces 0.06999 and 0.07411. The exploratory column (360 networks) is descriptive only. The H2 contrast was 0.061 in the exploratory set and 0.030 in the confirmatory set.
- **Figure 4.** Panels a–b plot the common slope with the mean intercept, limited to the observed C range (max 0.75). Panel c shows the two subset slopes that form the H2 contrast.
- **Figure 5.** Ablation gains, confirmatory cohort (C = 0: n = 107; C > 0: n = 213).

## Section 7
- **Table 7.** MAE_D, violation rate (tol 0.05) and excess for all seven models, plus the H3 contrast row. EN-36 has the lowest mean MAE_D (0.0306). EN-36-main and OLS have lower *median* MAE_D (0.0045, 0.0049 vs 0.0065) and lower violation rates (0.0118, 0.0122 vs 0.0362): pairwise products buy accuracy but add local-effect error at some activities.
- **Figure 6.** Pilot networks j9040_3, j902_5 and j3025_2 (pre-specified illustrative rule). Each network shows one activity, chosen by the largest range of the exact PD slope, with ties broken by PD range. The choice does not depend on model performance. The EN partial dependence is linear in each activity by construction (pairwise products are linear in each input), so its slope is constant. EBM's slope is jagged because its shape functions are piecewise constant. fastai's averaged curve follows the exact one, yet fastai has the worst scenario-level MAE_D (0.119). PD curves therefore do not substitute for scenario-level local fidelity; the caption says so.
- **Table 8 / Figure 7.** Decision test, k = 1–3. Shares are ratios of mean benefits. Negative losses (EBM at k = 1) arise because exact greedy is not optimal. Over the 160 networks with exhaustive search, the mean exhaustive − exact-greedy gap is 1.8e-5 (max 0.0088). Figure 7a intervals: network bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261122. CI static is omitted from panel a (loss 0.12–0.23).

## Exploratory structure measures
`model_evaluation/analysis/structure_measures_360.csv` holds exploratory structure measures for the 360 benchmark networks (C, number of critical sets, N_eff, decomposition terms and the RelMSE of EN-36, EBM-24 and fastai-41). It was produced during the exploratory phase that preceded protocol v1.0. Only `subset`, `C` and the three RelMSE columns are used here. C follows the definition implemented in the `structure` stage of `scripts/sections67`, which reproduces these 360 values. The file is descriptive and was not used for any confirmatory test.
